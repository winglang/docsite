Wing was built by the team at Wing Cloud (formerly Monada), a company founded by
[Elad Ben-Israel](https://github.com/eladb) and [Shai Ber](https://github.com/ShaiBer),
together with a community of contributors from around the world.

Wing Cloud has since shut down. The language specification, toolchain and local simulator
are released under the MIT license and remain free to use, and Wing continues as a
community-maintained open source project on [GitHub](https://github.com/winglang/wing).
